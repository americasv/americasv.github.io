Ramsey County: the places that honor veterans

We count at least 28 in Ramsey County: memorials, parks, posts named for those who served, and highways that carry their names. Here are the 27 you can visit, in a route of 18 stops and about 40 miles, from the county seat. Open it in Google Maps a few stops at a time, or go to any one of them.
