Washington County: the places that honor veterans

We count at least 22 in Washington County: memorials, parks, posts named for those who served, and highways that carry their names. Here are the 19 you can visit, in a route of 15 stops and about 80 miles, from the county seat. Open it in Google Maps a few stops at a time, or go to any one of them.

    Stillwater Veterans Memorial
    Stillwater
    Erected by the citizens of Washington County, 1916, on the grounds of the Historic Courthouse. In honor of all Washington County veterans, dedicated July 4, 2008. A Wall of Honor for the more than 100 students of Stillwater-area schools who died in war; dedicated Memorial Day 2004.
    101 Pine St W, Stillwater
    Directions

    A flag that once flew over the U.S. Capitol, placed over Main Street as a memorial to all veterans.
    Main St N, Stillwater

On the road

I-94, the Purple Heart Trail and Blue Star Memorial Highway, through Woodbury, Oakdale, Lake Elmo, Afton.

I-35, the 34th Infantry (Red Bull) Division Highway and Blue Star Memorial Highway, through Forest Lake.

U.S. 61, the Disabled American Veterans Highway, through Newport, St. Paul Park, Cottage Grove.
