Pine County: the places that honor veterans

We count at least 6 in Pine County: memorials, parks, posts named for those who served, and highways that carry their names. Here are the 4 you can visit, in a route of 4 stops and about 29 miles, from the county seat. Open it in Google Maps a few stops at a time, or go to any one of them.

Stops 1–4 in Google Maps

The line on the map joins the stops in order; Google Maps finds the roads.

  1. 1
    War memorials, old Pine County Courthouse
    Pine City
    Stones for the Civil War, both world wars, Korea, Vietnam and Desert Storm, in front of the 1939–40 courthouse.
    315 Main St S, Pine City
    Directions
  2. 2
    Master Sergeant Daniel L. Fedder Post Office
    Pine City
    Named by Congress in 2012 for MSgt Daniel L. Fedder, a Marine bomb-disposal technician from Pine City, killed in Afghanistan in 2010.
    170 Evergreen Square SW, Pine City
    Directions
  3. 3
    American Legion Heath-Perkins Post 51
    Pine City
    The Legion post of Pine City; both names are on its front.
    525 Main St N, Pine City
    Directions
  4. 4
    Sandstone Area Veterans Memorial
    Sandstone
    In honor and in memory of all who served in the Armed Forces of the United States.
    Sandstone
    Directions

On the road

Veterans Evergreen Memorial Drive (MN-23), through Askov, from I-35 at Banning State Park on toward Duluth.
