Sherburne County: the places that honor veterans

We count at least 17 in Sherburne County: memorials, parks, posts named for those who served, and highways that carry their names. Here are the 14 you can visit, in a route of 12 stops and about 56 miles, from the county seat. Open it in Google Maps a few stops at a time, or go to any one of them.

  1. 1
    Big Lake Veterans Memorial
    Big Lake Freedom Rock
    Big Lake
    Dedicated May 2017, at U.S. 10 and County 25; 244 names, four killed in action. A 31-ton boulder painted by Ray “Bubba” Sorensen: a draped flag, an eagle with a Purple Heart, Arthur Embretson of the USS Cyclops and Gold Star mother Stella Huso, ringed by pavers with the names of those who served.
    Veterans Memorial Park, 10 Lake St S, Big Lake
