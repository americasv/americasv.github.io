    American Legion Lee Goldsmith Post 201
    Mora
    Granite honor walls and a marker with the 34 men of the county killed in the world wars, Korea and Vietnam; built 2022–2024. The Legion post of Mora. With Ogilvie Post 640, it built the county memorial.
