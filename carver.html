Carver County: the places that honor veterans

We count at least 12 in Carver County: memorials, parks, posts named for those who served, and highways that carry their names. Here are the 11 you can visit, in a route of 7 stops and about 53 miles, from the county seat. Open it in Google Maps a few stops at a time, or go to any one of them.
