Wright County: the places that honor veterans

We count at least 13 in Wright County: memorials, parks, posts named for those who served, and highways that carry their names. Here are the 12 you can visit, in a route of 11 stops and about 98 miles, from the county seat. Open it in Google Maps a few stops at a time, or go to any one of them.

This QR code is on page 1 of the Wright County chapter of America's Silent Valor. It opens this map, with directions to each place: routes.americasv.org/wright
